Training Programs

Beginner Development

Build strong fundamentals, cricket awareness, basic hand-eye coordination and sports confidence in an engaging environment.

  • Correct grip, stance & balance
  • Basic catching & throwing mechanics
  • Soft leather & safety ball drills
  • Fun tactical mini-matches

  • Power hitting & timing mechanics
  • Spin deciphering (Doosra, Googly)
  • Mental discipline & innings building

Growth Journey

Player Development Process

A systematic 5-stage pathway designed to take an aspiring cricketer from baseline assessment to high-confidence match performance.

01

Assess

Understand the player's current technical ability, biomechanics, fitness baseline, and natural strengths.

02

Train

Build technical and physical skills through structured drills, repetitive muscle memory, and coach correction.

03

Practice

Apply skills through structured center-wicket match scenarios, pressure nets, and target bowling sessions.

04

Perform

Compete with confidence in inter-academy matches, district leagues, and club tournaments.

05

Progress

Track development using video analytics and metrics, promote to advanced squads, and continue improving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Shashi Cricket Academy, gear requirements, and training sessions.

We welcome young boys and girls starting from 6 years of age in our Beginner Development Squad. Training for junior ages (6-11) is designed to build foundational motor skills, hand-eye coordination, balance, and cricket enthusiasm with soft leather and safety equipment.

For trial sessions, the academy provides all necessary equipment including bats, balls, pads, and helmets. Once enrolled, coaches will advise on age-appropriate cricket kit requirements, and the academy provides full support with gear selection and bat preparation.
